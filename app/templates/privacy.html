Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

Data Collection & Usage

Kom-Way collects only your device's geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude). This data is used exclusively to fetch real-time weather forecasts relevant to your current location.

Data Storage & Retention

We do not store your location data. Requests are processed in real-time, and no personally identifiable information (PII) or location history is logged, stored, or retained on our servers.

Third-Party Data Sharing

To provide accurate weather information, your location coordinates are securely transmitted to third-party weather data providers. We currently utilize industry-standard weather service APIs to retrieve this information. We do not share your data with any other third parties, and we ensure that our providers receive only the minimum information necessary to fulfill your weather request.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy periodically to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the Kom-Way service, please contact us at cyclingdatahub@gmail.com.
