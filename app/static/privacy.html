Privacy Policy

Last updated: June, 2026

Data Collection

This application does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). Location data is used solely for the purpose of fetching local weather information and is not transmitted to any third-party databases outside of the necessary API calls to provide you with the weather service.

Weather Service

Weather data is retrieved based on your device's current location. This location data is processed locally on your device and sent only to the weather data provider to fetch relevant meteorological information.
